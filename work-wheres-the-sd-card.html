Where’s the SD card?

A graphic world of saturated planes and theatrical contrast.

RoleProduction Design · Director
Color languageOrange-red · green · black

Production design and directing in a visually heightened narrative space. Bold color fields frame the performers and organize the environment.

Visual concept

Space as a graphic composition.

Orange-red planes contrast with green walls and dark voids. Furniture, papers and framed surfaces give the strongly colored environment an everyday physical scale.

From concept to set

Building the frame.

Visual references and moodboards informed the set and prop choices. Coordination with the team carried those choices into the physical space, followed by set dressing on shoot days. The wider views reveal how the colored planes and furniture form a working set around the performers.
