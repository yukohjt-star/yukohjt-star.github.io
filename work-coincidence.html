Coincidence

Everyday spaces with a heightened sense of color.

Watch the filmSound on · Full screen available
RoleProduction Design · Director
Color languageAmber · red · blue · green

Production design and directing brought together through character, objects and contrasting spaces. A warm bar interior meets cooler working environments and bold graphic color.

Visual concept

Color gives each space a voice.

Amber glass and warm practical light give the bar a layered, intimate quality. Elsewhere, blue, red, orange and green create a sharper visual rhythm. Everyday objects connect the characters to these distinct environments.

Design approach / On set

Contrast with intention.

Bold complementary colors and balanced compositions shape the project’s visual approach. The practical details carry that language into each location: graphic orange and green table surfaces, curated wall art, and carefully arranged drinks and glassware.
