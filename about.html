Visual Development

I’m Jingting He, a Los Angeles–based production designer and art director working across narrative projects, commercial art departments and visual development.

Across my own narrative projects, I combine production design and directing. In visual development, I design cinematic worlds and use AI as a tool to visualize and execute them.

My experience includes prop sourcing, location scouting, cross-department coordination and FilmLA permitting. I work in Mandarin Chinese and English.
