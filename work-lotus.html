Lotus

From open desert
to a period encampment.

Set Builder · October 2026 · Period narrative film

My roleSet Builder
ScopeExterior encampment
Interior tent sets

As part of the art department, I helped build the encampment from an empty desert location: tents, fencing, entrance structures and practical scenic elements. The interior tent environments were also constructed from the ground up. Strong winds made the exterior build especially demanding.

01 / In the frame

A constructed world.

Production-monitor photographs show the exterior and interior sets through the camera. The outer monitor surrounds have been cropped for a closer view of the set.

02 / The starting point

Before the build.

The original desert location before the period encampment was constructed.

03 / Exterior construction

Tents, fencing and an entrance.

The build brought structure and practical detail to the open landscape, with tents, fencing, an entrance frame and period dressing.

04 / Interior construction

Building inside the tent.

From the tent enclosure and floor to rugs, furniture, screens and tabletop objects, the interior was assembled into a complete period environment.
