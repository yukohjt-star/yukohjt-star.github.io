Seven

An uncanny domestic world, built in monochrome.

Watch the filmSound on · Full screen available
RoleProduction Design · Visual Development
Color languageBlack, silver and worn gray

A visual world shaped through environments, props, mood and cinematic language. Repeated faces, worn surfaces and intimate objects turn a familiar bedroom into an unsettling space.

Visual concept

The familiar, made strange.

Patterned wallpaper, a heavy bed frame and a collection of mannequin heads give the room its dense visual identity. Without color, the contrast between skin, fabric, glass and aged surfaces carries the atmosphere.

Design-led visualization

I designed the visual world, environments, props, mood and cinematic language, using AI as a visualization and execution tool. The frames above present that design work through the finished images.
