Jadon, Mia, Jessica and Alex

A late-night misunderstanding.
A world told through color.

Watch the film · 4 min 05 secSound on · Full screen available
RoleProduction Design · Director
Format / PaletteNarrative comedy
Red · blue · pink · green · purple

A man leaves home late at night, leading his wife to suspect an affair. The misunderstanding builds toward an unexpected, ironic revelation about his nighttime behavior.

Visual concept

Color as character.

A bold, high-contrast palette gives each space a distinct emotional register. Red, blue, pink, green and purple reflect different aspects of character, using the environment to support the comedy and the story’s shifts in perspective.

Environment & set dressing

Rooms with an inner life.

Wallpaper gives the bedroom a strong visual identity and keeps the background active on camera. Different colored shower curtains mark shifts in the character’s emotional state, making a practical dressing element part of the visual storytelling.
