The last human in the world

A domestic interior divided by blue and red.

RoleProduction Design · Director
Color languageDeep blue · red

A narrative project combining production design and directing. Familiar rooms take on a charged atmosphere through opposing fields of color.

Visual concept

Color across a threshold.

Blue defines the room’s shadows, while red transforms the doorway and corridor. A phone screen introduces an intimate, luminous prop within the darker environment.
