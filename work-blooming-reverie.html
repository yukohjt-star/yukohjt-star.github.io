Blooming Reverie

A dream between meadow and sea.

Music video · In post-production / VFX in progress

RoleProduction Designer
Sole production designer: Jingting He
Color languagePetal pink · lilac · soft yellow
Leaf green · sea blue

A music-video world shaped through floral dressing, playful props and outdoor environments. The project is currently in post-production, with visual effects still in progress. The images shown here are work-in-progress stills and on-set photographs.

Visual concept

A landscape made playful.

Meadow and shoreline share a soft, botanical palette. Flowers, painted shells and translucent hanging forms introduce a dreamlike layer to natural locations, connecting intimate object details with the wider performance spaces.

Props / Preparation / On set

From painted shell to frame.

Pink, lilac and white shell props bring the pastel palette onto the sand. The preparation photographs trace the objects from painting to placement, where shells and pearl-like accents form a textured arrangement around the performer.

Environment / Location dressing

A garden with an ocean memory.

Floral details and translucent, jellyfish-like ornaments extend the visual world into the trees. The relationship between branches, hanging objects and the performers gives the setting depth while leaving room for movement.

Shoreline / Atmosphere

Pastel into twilight.

The beach sequence carries the project’s soft colors into a broader horizon. These location photographs and work-in-progress frames show the shift from close material details to the ensemble in the landscape.
