The Alien AIGC Film

An enclosed interior against an unknowable night.

Watch the filmSound on · Full screen available
RoleProduction Design · Visual Development
Color languageMidnight blue · amber · green

A nocturnal visual world developed through the car, the surrounding landscape, handheld objects and a tightly controlled atmosphere. The cabin becomes the meeting point between character and environment.

Visual concept

A world beyond the glass.

Cold blue rain and forest darkness surround the warm pockets of light inside the car. The dashboard, wet windows and close framing compress the space; the road and headlights open it back out.

Design-led visualization

I designed the visual world, environments, props, mood and cinematic language, using AI as a visualization and execution tool. The frames above present that design work through the finished images.
